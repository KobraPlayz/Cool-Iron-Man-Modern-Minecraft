# Suits & Abilities

All suits are passive — equip the trigger item in the correct slot and the powers activate automatically.

---

## Mark II

> *"The first flight-capable suit. Built as a prototype to test propulsion. The chrome titanium alloy proved nearly indestructible — but icing at high altitude cut testing short."*

**Trigger:** Iron Chestplate in `chest` slot *(placeholder — will be replaced with a custom item)*
**Power ID:** `iron_arsenal:mark2`

### Abilities

| Ability | Effect | Visible in GUI |
|---|---|---|
| Propulsion Flight | Enables directional Iron Man-style flight | Yes |
| Titanium-Chrome Alloy Armor | +20 armor points | Yes |
| Enhanced Strength | +6 attack damage | Yes |
| Impact Protection | Immune to fall damage | Yes |
| *(armor toughness)* | +10 armor toughness | Hidden |
| *(knockback resistance)* | Full knockback immunity | Hidden |
| *(heroic flight type)* | Enables prone Iron Man flight pose | Hidden |

### Notes

- Flight is always-on when the suit is worn. You choose whether to actually fly.
- Repulsors and Unibeam are planned but pending beam renderer schema confirmation.
